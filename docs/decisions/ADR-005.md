# ADR-005: Policy Engine & Action Gateway Isolation

## Context
Payment processing platforms require absolute guarantees that high-impact actions (blocking accounts, holding payments) are compliant with legal, business, and security policies.

## Decision
We decouple AI recommendations from action execution by inserting a deterministic **Policy Engine** and signed **Action Gateway**.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Mathematical enforcement of business guardrails; zero possibility of LLM rogue execution.
- **Negative:** Requires explicit policy definition for all potential response actions.
